# Historical CSV research

The separate CSV contains 46 nonblank research rows from 29 October 2026, not a current prospect or job list. One forum-sourced row was excluded; all remaining source rows are preserved as historical observations. Personal rank/priority columns are absent. Individual identity/email fields are omitted without discarding the remaining company/listing research. Source check dates are historical, not refreshed. Anonymous listing identities, rates, availability, geography and assignment conditions remain unverified where stated. Funding and jobs are not buying intent. A mailbox or application page is not permission to contact or create an account. No delivery validation is supplied.

The original ranked CSV and later fresh-leads variant are not replaced by or equated with this selected extract. General mailbox/source fields in this sheet do not establish a relationship with the owner. Source URLs remain references, not approved recipients.
