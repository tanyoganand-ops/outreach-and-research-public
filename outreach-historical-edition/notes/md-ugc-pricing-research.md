# UGC pricing research

Historical source date: 27 September and 1 October 2026. Re-edited 16 November 2026. Not refreshed. Prices, terms, availability, interface labels and requirements are historical claims, not an offer, eligibility decision or permission to act. Source quality and gaps are retained.

## 2026-09-27 16:56 - first answer
- UGC (brand posts the video): followers barely matter. UK beginner £50-£150/video, median £150-£250. The source proposed ~£150 for an AI niche and a ~£100 minimum; that minimum was explicitly revised in the later entry below.
- Historical market category, not a reader profile: a sponsored post at a nano tier (~1k followers) was estimated at ~£20-£150/post.
- Usage rights: +30-50% if the brand runs it as a paid ad.
Sources: https://whito.co.uk/research/influencer-ugc-rates-uk/ and https://fueler.io/blog/how-much-do-ugc-creators-earn-in-the-uk

## 2026-10-01 - revised (checked 1 Oct)
- Beginner UK evidence supports £50-£150, not a universal £100 minimum. The earlier "never below £100" was too rigid.
- Illustrative scope/pricing hypothesis, not a standing quote or observed market rate: £100 for one edited 20-45s demo with captions, one hook, one revision, limited organic use. £270 for three if batched. £75 can be fair for a tightly scoped first paid pilot. £150 is a target with good samples, not guaranteed.
- Paid-ad rights: +30-50% for 90 days (agency suggestion). Price raw footage, extra hooks, exclusivity and creator-account ad permissions separately. Avoid perpetual rights.
- JoinBrands UK: £50 default floor for UGC video, 20% free-tier fee, so £40 net at £50. Social-post video default £80 (different deliverable).
- Rates are self-reported or agency-reported, not a census, and not AI-specific.
