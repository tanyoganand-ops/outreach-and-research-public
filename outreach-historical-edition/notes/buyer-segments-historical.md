## What this evidence does not prove

The scan found hiring/listing signals in six UGC segments and earlier RFPs plus aggregator-listed tender volume in one design segment. These observations do not establish active spending in every segment, willingness to hire a freelancer instead of an employee/agency, lower freelance cost, or any reader's delivery capability. Employer salaries and vendor funding are not price anchors for freelance packs.

The source treated Amazon/Shopify listing-video services as weak evidence because, on 1 November 2026, the material it found was supplier marketing including a +23.8%sales claim, not buyer-side requests. That supplier claim is not independently verified and should not be repeated as a promised outcome.

## Optional concept tests, not accepted offers

- Property: an original property-tour-style video filmed in an authorised space, with no promised enquiries, viewings or sales.
- Clinic: a 20-40 second treatment-explainer format, using only lawful, substantiated claims and suitable footage. Confirm the real brief's on-camera conditions; no medical before/after claim is presumed acceptable.
- Hospitality: an original food-prep/plating or behind-scenes clip, with filming permission and a spec/concept label. Use real documented results if requested; do not invent them.
- Charity/professional body: a self-initiated homepage concept, never presented as commissioned client work. Actual tenders require separate deadline/eligibility checks.

Wedding/event content needs venue permission. Fitness and DTC examples are hypothetical formats, not proof of buyer interest. Contracts should state deliverables, organic/paid use, duration, revision scope and fee. No outcome, reach or revenue guarantee is supported here.
