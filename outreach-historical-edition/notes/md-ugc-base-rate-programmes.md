# UGC programmes with a stated or possible base rate

Historical source date: 4 October 2026. Re-edited 16 November 2026. Not refreshed. Prices, terms, availability, interface labels and requirements are historical claims, not an offer, eligibility decision or permission to act. Source quality and gaps are retained.

**Source set:** historical UGC application master list and ease-of-entry research dated 4 October 2026. No application priority is implied. This extract reports what those documents said, not fresh verification of programme terms or intake; no verified programme count is asserted.

"Base" means the programme description identifies fixed pay for a video, post, deliverable, campaign, or period, rather than relying only on views, referrals, or sales. Amounts and conditions are kept as described. A listed fee is not a guarantee of being selected or assigned work. Pure performance/affiliate-only programmes are excluded. Where the evidence does not establish a fixed base, the route is in **Ambiguous / unknown** rather than guessed into a base-pay category.
