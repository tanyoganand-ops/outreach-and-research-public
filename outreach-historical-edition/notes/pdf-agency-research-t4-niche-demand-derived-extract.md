### AI-generated UGC is changing the market 
- Arcads (Paris, 2024) raised USD 16M seed on 17 Dec 2025 and claims 6,000+ clients. 
FETCHED, company's own announcement. 
https://www.arcads.ai/blog/arcads-raises-16m-seed. A third-party teardown claims USD 15M 
ARR in 18 months (SNIPPET only, https://shuttergen.com/research/arcads-growth-teardown, 
16 May 2026, treat as unverified). 
- Counter-signal: Biddyco (an agency, so it has a motive) says brands are pulling back from 
AI UGC: low price per video but poor spend per winning ad, avatars look alike, comments 
call them fake, and trust drops. FETCHED. 4 Oct 2026. 
https://www.biddyco.com/blog-posts/ai-ugc-ads 
- UK rule: the ASA has no blanket AI disclosure duty. The test is whether the ad misleads, 
and a synthetic "customer" endorsement that does not exist is the misleading part. Source is 
a vendor blog quoting ASA guidance, FETCHED but secondary: 
https://www.versely.studio/blog/asa-has-no-blanket-ai-disclosure-rule. Check the ASA's own 
page before relying on this. UNVERIFIED against the primary source. 
- The source proposed AI-assisted production with real people rather than fictional customer endorsements. This is an untested strategic suggestion, not a demonstrated service capability or proof of performance.

medicines cannot be advertised to the public (CAP rule 12.12, HMR 2012). ASA says 18 
rulings since 2025 on these services. SNIPPET (not opened): 
https://www.asa.org.uk/news/protecting-people-from-harmful-ads-for-weight-loss-medicines-
new-research-and-enforcement-report.html and 
https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/weight-control-prescription-only-medicines.html (29 
May 2026). 
- Food supplements: ASA also has a rulings and guidance track (children's supplement 
ruling). SNIPPET: 
https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/birling-shore-ltd-g25-1323444-birling-shore-ltd.html 
- Health and wellness is also where the historical leads were most regulated (THG/ASDA weight 
management brief, T3 L21; wellness gifted collab L26; Stride L38). Separate claims and compliance checks are needed; no personal priority is inferred.
