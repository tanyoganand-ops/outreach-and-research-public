# Blank outreach and event-follow-up templates

Adapted from historical templates dated October 2026. These are blank examples, not sent messages or instructions to use a named recipient. The old event/deadline is not carried forward as upcoming. Resolve the actual person, date, relationship and permission before use. Do not invent age, school, location, client work, application status, reach or results. Replace every bracket and omit any claim that is not true. Review actual words before sending.

## Short connection request

Hi [Name], I'm [name]. I work on [truthful one-line topic]. Your [specific public work, accurately described] raised a question about [specific topic]. Would you be open to a short chat? Thanks, [name].

## Email asking for a short conversation

Subject: A question about [specific topic]

Hi [Name],

I'm [name], working on [accurate project description]. I read [specific work] and wanted to ask [one focused question]. Would you be open to a short conversation, or would a brief question by email be easier?

Thanks,
[name]
[relevant real link, if useful]

## Follow-up after an event actually attended

Subject: Thanks for [event/talk]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for [specific point they actually made] at [event and date]. It changed how I'm thinking about [truthful point]. Could I send one short question about [topic]?

Thanks,
[name]

## Honest sample-based service introduction

Hi [Name],

I noticed [accurate, relevant company observation]. I have an idea for [specific deliverable], using [real sample or proposed approach]. [If the sample is unpaid concept work, state that plainly.] Would it be useful if I sent a short outline?

Thanks,
[name]

For actual commercial email, these bodies are not a complete compliance package. Check the applicable lawful basis, identification, postal-address and opt-out requirements as well as the recipient/source and approved words. A company mailbox is not blanket consent. No fee, deliverable, consent, licence or meeting is agreed by an example.
