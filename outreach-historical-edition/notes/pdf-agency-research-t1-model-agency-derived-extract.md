## General process lessons, not a team plan

Written scope, creator permissions, client approval, usage period, paid-ad/account-based ad permissions and a real reporting rhythm are useful contract/process questions. A case study must use actual work/results and authorised client marks. A labelled original concept can illustrate technique, not imply a client commission, testimonial or existing roster.

This is a business-model comparison, not a chosen operating plan or capability claim. No permission to copy scripts, creative assets, music or people's likeness follows from observing a format. Ads need clear disclosure and substantiated claims; no undisclosed clipper network, inflated metrics or product-evasion claim is endorsed.
