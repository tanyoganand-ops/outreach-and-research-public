# Free-perks v20: agency software terms, not headline months

Historical source date: 16 October 2026. Re-edited 16 November 2026. Not refreshed. Prices, terms, availability, interface labels and requirements are historical claims, not an offer, eligibility decision or permission to act. Source quality and gaps are retained.

Source-page research recorded on 16 October 2026. Specific assets and account eligibility are not assessed.

Notion is a more plausible service-business lead than several larger startup offers, but its own help says agencies/consultancies and teams below ten receive one month, not the advertised six. The legal terms also exclude ever-paid customers, a stricter test than "not at the source date paying". Miro excludes consulting/service providers and charges the card beyond credit. JetBrains' direct offer is a discount requiring spend; six free months is a different partner route. Product-CAD and non-commercial VFX tools are not safe substitutes for client-production licences.

## 1. Notion: the exact offer is one, three or six months

Official source-date help: https://www.notion.com/en-gb/help/notion-for-startups

Source-date landing/FAQ: https://www.notion.com/startups

Full contract reached from the help page: https://notion.notion.site/Notion-for-Startup-Terms-936b74c0323745a186b1497747074020

Source-date breakdown:

- Partner-affiliated qualifying startups: up to six months Business including Notion AI.
- Qualifying non-partner startups: three months.
- SMBs/service businesses, agencies, consultancies and teams under ten: one month according to the source-date FAQ. Incomplete business verification can also reduce an award to one month.

Three/six-month routes are aimed at scalable technology-related products/services, not every new business using software. A company website, company-domain work email, fewer than 100 staff and qualifying partner evidence where relevant are published requirements. A partner's public code is not proof that an applicant is affiliated with it; 

### Details that make claiming too early costly

- The upgrade takes effect immediately when approved. The benefit belongs to a particular workspace and cannot be transferred after application.
- Only one promotional redemption; offers/codes do not stack.
- Source-date full legal terms say the recipient is at the source date non-paying **and has never previously been paying**, moving to a new Business plan. Help-page shorthand says "not at the source date on a paid plan". Do not treat cancelling an existing plan as a reset to eligibility.
- Existing Plus/Business/Enterprise subscriptions and historical invoices cannot be retroactively covered. No refund of earlier bills is promised.
- Legal terms refer to one-year expiry of the issued offer, but that does **not** turn a one/three/six-month service allowance into a free year. The period and application timing must be confirmed in the actual award.
- Verification information may be shared with a third-party provider under the programme terms.

General trial billing is separately documented here: https://www.notion.com/help/paid-plan-trials

That page says payment details entered for an ordinary Business trial lead to automatic paid renewal unless cancelled; without payment details, confirmation of upgrade is requested at the end. This does not prove every startup promotion uses identical mechanics. Inspect the actual offered workspace/billing screen, preserve the end date, and do not supply a card just to obtain a free month. 

Practical verdict: only useful when ready to test an actual workflow. Starting an expiring workspace benefit while files/usage are still undecided can waste it and complicate a later migration.

## 2. Linear: historical billing terms and partner conditions

Programme: https://linear.app/startups

Source-date billing docs: https://linear.app/docs/billing-and-plans

The programme landing page says free Business access but does not state its duration in the readable text. Its own source-date billing documentation supplies **up to six months**. Directories disagree about Basic versus Business and three versus six months; the source-date first-party description is Business, up to six months, with the particular award still controlling.

Only startups affiliated with an official programme partner can participate. It is not a universally accessible public code. The landing page also describes a free plan with unlimited members, 250 issues and unlimited archived issues, which may be the lower-commitment option without a promotional application.

The full eligibility answers/end-of-benefit mechanics were not all available in the fetched landing text. Do not repeat an independent directory's "automatically downgrades" or "card starts billing" as a verified fact. Actual partner link/offer, workspace and actual account state need checking before acceptance. 

## 3. JetBrains: separate the paid discount from partner free months

Official programme and FAQ: https://www.jetbrains.com/startups/

The direct Startup Program offers **50% off** commercial developer tools for up to five years. It is still a purchase. The partner programme is for accelerators, investors and startup communities; qualifying affiliated startups can get six months free for up to ten total licences, followed by the standard startup discount for the remainder of the term.

Published direct-route criteria include proof the company was established within the past five years. Source-date FAQ discusses limits on IDE, AI, team/learning and plugin subscriptions. Some FAQ wording describes limits over enrollment, while another answer says per-year purchases. Do not budget licences from that inconsistency: an actual written quote/award should settle quantities and renewal prices.

Existing paid invoices are not automatically refunded/credited. Parent/subsidiary relationships affect one-time eligibility. If genuinely partner-affiliated, the operator recommends looking at that route before directly taking the discount. 

Practical verdict: keep in a **discounts** section unless a verified partner free-period grant exists. A 50% saving on software not otherwise needed has no free-perk value.

## 4. Miro: credit is not a hard cap, and service businesses are excluded

Full official help and programme terms: https://help.miro.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014912819-Miro-Startup-Program

Partner-linked startups can receive $1,000; individual startups can be considered for $500. Published criteria include no service provider/consultancy business, no paid subscription membership and startup/domain/funding conditions. The embedded eligibility terms are stricter on account history: no active, expired **or trial** paid-subscription administrator/member. Do not begin a paid trial while waiting for a programme application.

The credit must be activated within six months. It is linked to the email in the application, not any colleague who later upgrades. Upgrading as the wrong user can cause an unwanted card charge. Purchases above the credit cost money immediately; more members consume the credit faster. When credit is exhausted, the card starts being charged.

The official example shows a 20-seat annual plan exceeding a $1,000 grant and requiring a cash difference, rather than providing a free year. Programme participants also permit name/logo identification and case study/testimonial participation if requested. 

## 5. SOLIDWORKS: reseller headline versus actual product-startup scope

Maker's source-date eligibility: https://www.solidworks.com/solution/solidworks-for-startups-program

UK reseller offer: https://www.solidsolutions.co.uk/solidworks-for-startups/

The reseller describes a free first year, then 70% off year two and 50% off year three, with an option to downgrade/end. It advertises a broad portfolio of CAD, simulation, visualisation and cloud tools. That is a real offer for approved participants, not proof all freelancers qualify.

The maker's criteria are a physical product being designed/built to sell, less than **$1M revenue** and at most five years old. Existing customers at the historical source date, service providers, engineering/design firms, consultants and contract manufacturers are excluded. A reseller page elsewhere mixes dollar and pound thresholds and talks broadly about CAD technicians; the maker's specific exclusions take precedence over promotional wording. Companies at $1M-$5M revenue/funding may be considered for a **paid discounted** scale-up offer instead.

This is not a free general-purpose render/agency licence. Application-specific seats, exports, hardware, commercial scope, second-year renewal/cancellation and the actual contract remain unverified. 

## 6. Foundry: free practice can create non-portable projects

Source-date official FAQ: https://www.foundry.com/non-commercial-faqs

Nuke Non-commercial and Mari Non-commercial are free, non-watermarked but restricted learning/experimentation licences. They are **not** for providing services to third parties or other commercial work. Personal demo reels are a distinct permitted case, not permission for client delivery. Recreational prize competitions have limits; competitions whose prize is future commercial development and certain revenue-linked festivals are excluded.

Two practical migration traps:

- Nuke Non-commercial stores encrypted .nknc/.gznc/.hroxnc data that commercial Nuke cannot load. It is not a seamless free start followed by a paid project upgrade. Output is limited to 1920x1080, with plugin/codec/scripting limitations.
- Mari Non-commercial .mnc projects cannot load into commercial Mari; texture exports are capped at 4K with other restrictions.

A Foundry account and online device activation are needed. Licences roll thirty days from the most recent connected use and have device limits. Keep learning files separate from commercial production originals.

## 7. New Diorama laptop: useful future lead, autumn application closed

Official offer and full practical conditions: https://newdiorama.com/for-artists/universal-basic-assets

The page advertises communal onsite MacBook Pro access with AutoCAD, QLab, Logic, Capture and Adobe, desk space, storage, drinks and tickets, in one-to-three-week blocks. It is for freelance theatre designers developing new theatre/performance work, with most working hours onsite; not full-time salaried designers. Laptop remains onsite, Monday-Friday 10:00-18:00. Communal availability makes tight-deadline delivery unsuitable.

Search made it look open because usage dates are 19 October-18 December. The actual first-phase application deadline was **Thursday 1 October 2026 at 18:00**, outcomes due on 16 October 2026. That historical autumn round should not be presented as open. The page says another lottery launches in December for spring, but exact application dates and terms are not yet verified. 

## Keep benefit categories separate

"Free to apply", "discount", "free period", "credit", "free local production tool" and "non-commercial learning licence" are different categories. Before any selected route: reconcile actual accounts/redemptions, verify the exact final offer, preserve originals, inspect renewal/cancellation/export consequences and obtain the account owner's approval for signup or spending.
