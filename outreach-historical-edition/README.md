# Historical UGC, outreach and agency research

September-November 2026 research, selected and re-edited 16 November 2026. A historical reference collection, not a current opportunity list, legal advice, evidence of outreach, or a statement about any reader's age, residence, student status, audience, clients or results. Company marketing and third-party claims remain attributed or caveated. A provider requirement is not a fact about a reader.

## Start here

- Open `historical-research-indexed.pdf` for the complete 21-chapter selected text edition with a clickable contents page and bookmarks.
- `notes/` contains the same chapters as editable Markdown.
- `trackers/historical-prospect-research-2026-10-29.csv` is a separate 46-row historical research extract. Its scope/limitations are in `trackers/README.md`. It is not a sent/application tracker.

## What is included

Blank outreach examples; general company contact routes; dated UGC programme/pay observations; historical application field notes; agency/niche/buyer-signal comparisons; generic production and phone-editing notes; app-listing/privacy/terms conflicts; software, hosting and media-source licence research. Each chapter gives a historical source date. No particular version is designated latest.

## What is not included

Original working records, private biography/project/account/network context, filled applications, unverified claims of completed work, individual contact profiles or named-person emails, unpublished decisions, source evidence/diffs, media assets, renders, reference screenshots, source ZIPs and unselected mixed PDFs/spreadsheets are not included. This is a selected edition, not all historical work or a claim that every excluded file was unsafe. Overlapping extracts are thematic selections, not substitutes declared identical to every variant. No licence is granted for third-party media merely mentioned in research; this collection includes no such media.

## Before real use

All prices, programme terms, deadlines, eligibility, routes, interface labels and law/platform guidance are historical. No blanket live-URL refresh was performed. A link is a source reference, not an approved recipient or consent to contact. Recover the current official source before acting. Confirm rights/consent, actual account and applicant details, exact cost/conditions and message wording. Never invent biography, portfolio results, clinical/financial/legal outcomes, or signatures/consent. A brief cannot override law, platform disclosure or media rights. Blank examples do not authorise a message, application, account, purchase or post.
