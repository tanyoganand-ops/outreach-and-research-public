# Verified outside-supplier design RFPs

Clearwater Studios, web and product design leads. Checked 30 October 2026 at the buyers' own pages and RFP documents. Research only: nothing sent, applied for, registered or submitted. Adds to v2 and is separate from the unverified tender signals.

## Result

Two live requests for proposals were verified at the buyer's own site, each asking an outside firm for web or UX design. Both are small non-profit or regional-body projects with stated requirements, so the entry bar is readable. Neither is a warm lead or a sign of interest in Clearwater. One further RFP checked has closed, and two others were excluded (reasons below). Whether Clearwater can meet the stated requirements is not assessed beyond what the documents say.

## 1. Centralina Economic Development District (North Carolina, USA): website redevelopment and communications support

- Ask: Redevelop the CEDD website (currently WordPress/Elementor): streamlined sitemap and visual design, accessible mobile-friendly site, project and initiatives directory, templates, content migration, analytics, staff training. Plus separately priced communications support: three digital newsletters a year and optional content writing.

- Dates: Issued 30 Oct 2026. Questions by 20 Nov. Proposal deadline: "November 30, 2026 at 5:00 PM EDT", quoted exactly from the cover page; the body text says only "due by November 30, 2026". The document does not say which time zone it intends. EDT would normally have ended on 1 Nov 2026, so the "EDT" label may be loose. The buyer should confirm the exact time. Selection and kickoff mid-December 2026.

- How to respond: One PDF by email to the named administrator, subject "CEDD Website Redevelopment Proposal". About 3-5 pages preferred. No speculative designs requested.

- Stated requirements: Firm overview and contact; two examples of relevant websites (links are sufficient); recommended approach and platform; who does content, copywriting and migration; accessibility, testing, training and support; separate pricing; one or two client references; assumptions. Award is subject to the buyer's contracting, insurance and approval requirements.

- Not stated: No budget figure. No turnover, minimum years in business, or firm-size requirement. No location restriction found in the document, and none either way for non-US firms: not confirmed with the buyer.

- What it asks beyond design: Copywriting, WordPress migration, newsletter production and hosting/maintenance pricing. Design alone would not cover it, so a bid would need those capabilities or a partner.

- Sources: Buyer notice page and RFP document, both at centralinaedd.org. The whole RFP was read.

## 2. Asia Pacific Foundation of Canada (APF Canada): UX review and redesign recommendations for asiapacific.ca

- Ask: An independent, evidence-based UX review of asiapacific.ca with prioritised recommendations on audiences, information architecture, search and filtering for a large publications archive, and a modernised visual direction. A strategic redesign engagement; explicitly not an implementation contract. RFP ref. APFC-2026-UX-01.

- Dates: Issued 22 Oct 2026. Written questions due 2 Nov 2026, 5:00 p.m. PT. Answers posted to all proponents 6 Nov 2026. Proposals due 23 Nov 2026, 5:00 p.m. PT (the RFP text also says "Pacific Time"). About 14-18 weeks from kickoff. Times are the source's labels, not converted.

- Budget: Not to exceed C$30,000, inclusive of fees, participant incentives, tools, travel and taxes.

- Stated requirements: Proposal up to 20 pages. Named team with roles. Three comparable projects (ideally content-heavy, research-driven, mission-based, bilingual or public-sector) with client references. Portfolio showing research rigour and design quality, with measured outcomes where possible. French-language research capacity and accessibility expertise, including certifications or audit experience. User research must meet PIPEDA and provincial privacy law.

- Sources: APF Canada's own page (shows the RFP reference, dates and privacy requirement) and the full RFP text on its careers portal, which the organisation's own page ties to the same reference number and dates.

## 3. Checked and not included

- Open Technology Fund, website redesign: Closed. Proposals were due 23 Oct 2026. Own pages: RFP, FAQ. The FAQ says non-US firms, sole proprietors and subcontracted teams were eligible, which shows how that buyer treated small suppliers.

- UNICEF Kosovo, branding identity for an early childhood programme: Excluded: the document says the company must be licensed and registered in Kosovo. Deadline 6 Nov 2026. Own page.

- Search for Common Ground, ConnexUS brand and website: Not verified: the only source found is a job-board listing, and no page on the organisation's own site was found. Left out.

## 4. What this does and does not show

Both live items are explicit asks for an outside supplier, read at the owner's source. Dates, budget (APF only) and requirements above come from those documents.

Neither buyer has expressed interest in Clearwater. A proposal would be a cold, formal submission.

Both ask for relevant past work and client references, and APF also asks for research, French and accessibility credentials. The pack makes no claim that Clearwater meets them.

Open choices: whether to pursue either, whether to find a partner for the build or research parts, and whether to submit anything. Nothing has been decided or done.
