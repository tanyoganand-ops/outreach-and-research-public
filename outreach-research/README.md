# UGC, outreach and agency resources

Research packs, drafts, plans and lead data for a design and UGC studio and its outreach work (September to November 2026), gathered in one place.

## Read this first

- Everything here is research, plans, templates or drafts. Nothing here is proof that any message, application or pitch was sent. Checks of the records available found no sends.
- Every document is a dated snapshot. Leads, pay figures, programme terms, deadlines and event dates were not refreshed and may have changed or closed.
- Placeholders in [square brackets] are unfilled. Personal details (introduction, location, age, status, follower counts, reach figures) are placeholders or were removed. Nothing in this repo asserts anything about the author's age, status, location or reach. Programme age and student rules quoted here are the programmes' own.
- Business contact routes are ones the companies publish themselves. Use them lawfully (UK: PECR and UK GDPR for cold email and calls).
- Where near-identical versions exist, none is claimed to be the latest.
- Documents were machine-edited to remove personal and private details. Edits are redactions or neutral rewording only. Some redacted PDF pages show mixed fonts or small gaps.
- Withheld on purpose: personal LinkedIn profile and post URLs, named-person email addresses, estate-agent staff names and branch phone numbers. Role mailboxes (info@, hello@) published by companies are kept. Treat every contact field as unverified.
- Third-party media, video and image assets are not republished.

## Archival status of the lead data

Every status, rate, age or follower requirement, "verified-open", "listed-unverified" and similar label in csv-06 to csv-13 and the txt files is an archival claim as recorded on the date shown (mostly 23 Oct to 1 Nov 2026). None of it has been refreshed, and none is a freshly verified opportunity, an acceptance, or evidence that the author applied. Pay figures are the sources' own advertised claims.
