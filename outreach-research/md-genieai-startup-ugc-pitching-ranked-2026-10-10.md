## PART 2 - GenieAI: the deep dive

### What GenieAI is (verified)

- London legal-AI startup (Genie AI Ltd). Product: AI that drafts, reviews and negotiates contracts for business teams, 120+ jurisdictions. Source: https://www.genieai.co/
- Series A: $17.8M (£13.3M) announced 23 October 2024, led by GV (Google Ventures) with Khosla Ventures; total funding $20M+. Sources: https://www.genieai.co/blog/genie-ai-raises-17-8-million-to-build-agentic-legal-ai, https://www.genieai.co/our-backers, Bloomberg Law coverage
- Independent product reviews: Trustpilot 4.7 from 48 reviews (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/genieai.co), Product Hunt 17 reviews, SoftwareAdvice 4.5. Reviews are for the product, not the creator programme.
- This is a real, funded company with a real product. Not a CV-harvesting fake.

### Is the creator programme real?

The programme page is live, current (page content references 2026 throughout), and unusually detailed for a fake: exact fee, exact referral tiers, a real application form, a named creator dashboard, monthly bank-transfer payouts, and a "Genie Content Creators T&Cs apply" line. GenieAI is also hiring a separate "Legal Content Creator" role (marketing, fully remote, UK) listed via its careers page, which matches the programme existing as a real marketing motion.

What I could NOT verify: any independent creator saying "I got paid the $260". No Reddit/Trustpilot posts about the programme either way. Treat the terms as advertised-only. The T&Cs themselves are not public; they arrive in the creator kit after acceptance.

Mitigations built into the programme's own process: concepts are approved BEFORE you record (so a rejected video wastes no filming), there is no fee to apply, no "training fee", no minimum commitment, and you keep ownership of every video even if unpaid. Worst realistic case: one video made, not accepted, still owned and usable as a portfolio piece. That is a cheap test.

### Current advertised terms (read live 10 Oct 2026, https://www.genieai.co/genie-content-creators)

- $260 flat per published video accepted against an approved concept. No minimum, no cap, no tiers.
- Referral rewards on top, paid once per referred paying customer: $130 (Pro plan), $650 (Business), $1,300 (Enterprise intro that signs).
- Free GenieAI Pro account for active creators (advertised as worth $75/month), monthly briefs, brand assets/B-roll, referral links, creator dashboard, early feature access, verified public profile on genieai.co/creators, private creator Slack.
- Payment: monthly, bank transfer in your local currency. Submissions reviewed within 5 working days; accepted videos paid in the next monthly run; "most creators see their first payout within 30 days of their first submission" (their claim).
- Formats (6): platform tutorials; honest reviews; how-to guides; tips and tricks (<60s shorts); AI-and-business commentary; AI-and-society explainers. Publish on YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, X or your own platform; long-form 2-10 min or shorts both count.
- Quality bar: 1080p minimum, clean audio, captions on everything, show the real product not slides, feature claims checked against their docs.
- Honesty rules: disclose the partnership on every video (platform paid-partnership label + #GenieCreator); reviews stay honest ("we pay for the video, not the verdict"); never present a video as legal advice; never fabricate a result.
- Process: apply (decision within 5 working days) > get kit > pitch a concept and get it approved BEFORE recording > publish > submit link in dashboard within 14 days > review within 5 working days > one round of small fixes if needed > paid in next monthly run.
- Ownership: you keep your videos; GenieAI gets a licence to re-share, clip and boost with credit.

### The application (form inspected live)

The "Apply to become a Genie Creator" button opens a form asking only:
- Full name, email, primary channel URL
- Where you publish (YouTube / LinkedIn / TikTok / X / Instagram / Podcast / Other)
- Audience size across channels (a range dropdown)
- Formats you'd make (checkboxes matching the 6 formats)
- Optional: a video you're proud of, other channels, what you'd make first, how you heard about it
- One terms checkbox (programme terms + holding your details to assess the application)

No age question anywhere on the form or FAQ. Eligibility FAQ answer, verbatim: "Anyone who can make a genuinely good video. YouTubers and TikTok creators. LinkedIn voices. Lawyers and ops people who film. Students of AI. We screen on craft and clarity, not follower count."

1. A primary channel URL and one sample video link - the optional "video you're proud of" and "what you'd make first" fields are exactly where the delivered GenieAI spec sample (sample 1, the NDA video) plugs in.
3. Formats that match what he wants to make: the delivered spec sample maps to Format 4 (tips and tricks, <60s) and Format 3 (problem-first how-to).
4. UK disclosure law applies regardless of programme rules: paid content must be obviously labelled (ASA/CMA - see Part 4).

### GenieAI affiliate programme (backup route)

Separate from the creator programme: https://www.genieai.co/affiliate-program - 100% of the first month's subscription per referred customer, powered by FirstPromoter, free to join. Lower value than $260/video but has no acceptance bar. Not a substitute; an add-on link in the same videos.

## PART 4 - Pitching spec samples to startups: the playbook

### Who to pitch (targeting beats volume)

- Data (PitchBrand, from 29,000+ real creator outreach emails, https://www.pitchbrand.co/statistics/state-of-pitching-2026): tightly targeted campaigns (~35 contacts) averaged a 21.4% reply rate; 250+ brand blasts got 1.8%. Having "UGC" in the subject line replied at 4.8% overall.
- So: 5 carefully chosen AI startups a week, not 50 generic ones. Best targets: AI dev-tools and agent startups whose products the pitcher genuinely uses, roughly seed-to-Series-B, with no public creator programme (the programmes above cover the ones that have them).
- Where to find them: YC company list and Ashby job boards (a startup hiring "content creator" or "creator marketing" is buying UGC right now - Retell and Scrollmark both surfaced this way), Product Hunt launches, and the client lists of agencies like Creator Match.

### The email structure that converts (sourced)

From pitchlo.com, joinflare.app, ugcplayground.com and creatorsjet.com guides, cross-checked today:

1. Subject line: "UGC for [Brand] - quick idea" (or "[Brand] x 20-second demo video?"). Brand name in the subject, always.
2. Under 150 words total (over 150 and reply rates drop, per Flare's guide).
3. Four parts: (a) one credibility line - what you make and the niche ("I make short, honest 20-30s demo videos of AI dev tools"); (b) 2-3 relevant samples as one link (the spec-sample link from this morning's doc); (c) one specific offer ("a 25-second video showing [specific feature] solving [specific problem], delivered in 5 days"); (d) one easy CTA ("want a 2-line plan first?").
4. Send to a named person: brand manager, social media manager, growth/marketing lead, or the founder at small startups. Never info@.
5. Lead with their product, not your bio: one line proving you know what the product does (reference a feature or their current ads).
6. Do not quote rates in the first email unless they ask; have the rate card ready for the reply (below).
7. Follow up once, 3-4 days later, one line ("floating this back up - happy to send a 2-line plan for [feature]"). 26% of brand replies come from the follow-up, not the first email (PitchBrand). 42% of replies arrive within 60 minutes, so same-week follow-ups only.

### Rates for a first-timer (UK, sourced 10 Oct 2026)

- Beginner UGC video: £50-£150 per video (Velena UK UGC Rates Report 2026: https://velenalifestyle.com/ugc-rates-report-uk-2026/; fluxnote.io UK guide recommends starting £100-£150: https://fluxnote.io/guides/ugc-creator-rates-uk; Whito June 2026 benchmarks put a UGC video for a brand's own channels at £50-£250: https://whito.co.uk/research/influencer-ugc-rates-uk/)
- After 5-10 delivered projects: move to £200-£350 (fluxnote).
- Usage rights for paid ads: add 30-50% of base rate (Influee 2026 guide: https://influee.co/blog/ugc-price). Whitelisting (ads run from the creator's handle) is a separate add-on again (UGC Agent rate calculator: https://ugcagent.app/tools/ugc-rate-calculator/).
- House guidance from our earlier pricing research still holds: ~£150/video base for the AI niche, never under ~£100 - quote £100-£150 for direct startup pitches and only dip lower for volume bundles.
- Anchor against the programmes: GenieAI's $260 (~£205) flat fee is what a funded startup already pays for one accepted video - use that mentally when a startup tries to pay £30.
- Always agree in writing before filming: video count, length, usage rights (organic only vs paid ads, and for how long), revision rounds, payment amount and date. Never pay a "training fee" to anyone. Ever.

### Legal must-dos (UK)

- Any paid (or gifted) content must be obviously labelled as an ad - the platform's paid-partnership label and/or #ad. This is UK consumer-protection law, enforced by the CMA and Trading Standards, with the ASA naming non-compliant creators. Sources: https://www.asa.org.uk/advice-online/influencers-guide.html, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/social-media-endorsements-guidance-for-content-creators/social-media-endorsements-being-transparent-with-your-followers
- Spec samples stay labelled "spec sample - not sponsored" until a brand actually pays; from that moment they are ads and need the label.
- If the creator is under 18, programme contracts and payouts may need a parent/guardian involved - none of the application forms above ask age, but their T&Cs (delivered at acceptance) will govern it. Read them before the first paid video, not after.

### The 7-day sequence

- Day 1: apply to GenieAI (form, spec sample 1 linked), cognee (email), Lindy (Dub form) and Marcus (sign-in). Four applications, about 40 minutes total, all phone-doable.
- Day 1-2: record spec sample 1 (GenieAI NDA video) - it feeds the GenieAI application's "video you're proud of" field and is the strongest pitch asset.
- Day 3: send 5 direct pitches to AI startups using the spec-sample link and the 150-word structure.
- Day 6-7: follow up the 5 pitches once. Post one cognee-style dev-tool screenshot post (organic, real output) to start a 7-day impressions clock.
- Keep a simple tracker: programme/startup, date applied, status, terms offered, payment date. (Tracker template already exists in UGC-2-application-kit from 6 Oct.)
