Shape: creator is paid upfront with no platform protection on either side; the contract terms are whatever the two parties agree in chat.
Portfolio requirements (same page): at least two videos recommended - the marketplace card shows the first two; up to three package tiers with your own pricing, each stating video count, revisions and turnaround; response time and completed orders feed ranking.

## 5. What the verified detail supports (observations, then hypotheses)

Observations (narrow, dated, sourced above):
1. Real, current, UK-payable UGC briefs exist on a zero-commission board at £10-£250, from buyers whose own sites check out at first level. The platform's creator roster shows starting prices of £50-£220.
2. Two of four live briefs ask the creator to price usage rights explicitly; BindSafe carves paid-ad usage out for separate negotiation. Rights pricing is expected in real briefs - brief-level evidence, no rate range claimed.
3. PPH observed bid counts (22:40 table above; re-checked 23:14): the generic small fixed-price rows mostly showed 16-67 proposals (23:14 sample: $118 briefs at 49 and 56, $40 at 58, $287 at 26, $120 at 16); the three specialist rows at 22:40 showed 3, 13 and 7. These are observed visible bid counts on one board - reported as counts only. Brief ages differ, budgets mix GBP/USD unmatched across groups, and the specialist/generic split is not a matched comparison: no conclusion about specialist categories facing less competition is drawn from this data.
4. The strongest design engagement found tonight (Trellis) is day-shaped, tightly scoped, direct-hire, and explicitly wants structural case-study evidence - and excludes agencies.

Channel tradeoffs, verified on owning pages (payment risk / revisions / rights):
- ugccreators.co.uk: no escrow, no platform refund, brand pays creator's bank directly BEFORE work starts; revisions and turnaround are self-declared in the creator's package tiers; rights negotiated per brief (briefs ask for rights pricing). Lowest fee (zero) and highest payment control, but no platform protection and contract terms are whatever the chat agrees.
- PeoplePerHour: platform-intermediated flow with proposal competition visible; fees 20%/7.5%/3.5% per buyer lifetime billing (excl VAT) - steepest on the small fixed-price briefs that dominate the design board.
- Billo: platform-managed payouts via Stripe (weekly per help article, two-weekly per marketing page - unresolved discrepancy); add-on pricing for posting/ads rights; briefs in-app only.
- JoinBrands: payments inside the platform; content-only vs content+posting job types.
- Collabstr: storefront, creator-set per-deliverable prices, "instant payouts" (owning page); usage rights agreed with creator before payment.
- TikTok Shop UK Affiliate: commission-only on sales; no fixed fees at all.
- Fluencify: pay per campaign hidden until signup (opaque).

Hypotheses for you to test (none established):
- H1: Specialist design (3D/CAD/motion/interactive) may face less per-brief competition than generalist design on open boards. Tonight's counts are consistent with that and equally consistent with brief age, budget and niche-tooling confounders - the observed counts above are NOT evidence for it. Test: 2-4 weeks of PPH/Contract Spy tracking with brief age and budget alongside proposal counts.
- H2: A creator quote that prices usage rights explicitly reads as more professional to SMB buyers, because buyers are already asking. Test: include a rights line in future quotes and track responses.
- H3: Regulated-category UGC may pay more per creator (single closed THG/ASDA campaign at £100-£1,000 - one expired sample cannot establish a premium). Test: next regulated campaign, like-for-like deliverables.
- H4: Individual-freelancer-shaped applications (Trellis-style direct hire) may be a better fit for design deals than agency-shaped pitches, because the best-scoped brief tonight explicitly excluded agencies. Single data point - test by watching how Contract Spy digest briefs phrase their routes.
