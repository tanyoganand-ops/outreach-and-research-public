# Public listing and legal-page facts, and claims to avoid (checked 23 Oct 2026)

Draft from public pages only. These are not briefs. Track identity is still a hypothesis: Dare, Line and Areum are the likely tracks; ReGen and Willow are separate fourth-track candidates and neither is confirmed. Nothing was signed up for, uploaded, bought or tested in an app. Reviews are unverified user reports, never established prices. A brief cannot substantiate a disputed price or feature claim: verify numbers against the owner source and the real app or paywall on the day before any numeric claim goes in a video. Law and platform rules, then the brief, override this note.

## Dare (likely track)
- Listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dare-ai-photo-video/id6788347858 . Terms: https://trydare.com/legal/terms-of-service . Privacy: https://trydare.com/legal/privacy-policy (last updated 13 Oct 2026, read).
- Terms (Bounty Studio, Inc. d/b/a Dare) require permission for any person or voice in uploaded media.
- Privacy says: face data is collected only when you provide or create content with a face; no faceprints or biometric identifiers are created or stored; face data is not used to train Dare's own models; uploads and generations are not published to a public feed unless you make a project public and share its link. Dare advertises on Meta platforms and uses Meta Business Tools, but says it never shares prompts, uploads, generated media or face data with Meta.
- Avoid: showing or imitating a real person without permission; speed or quality claims you did not measure; saying anything about how your own media is stored beyond what you checked in the app.

## Line (likely track)
- Listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/line-learn-any-language/id6780760864 . Terms: https://app.tryline.ai/legal/terms (effective 13 Sep 2026). Privacy: https://app.tryline.ai/legal/privacy (effective 13 Sep 2026). Both read through a browser, because plain fetch returned empty.
- Terms say: AI output can be wrong, so treat it as practice material; free use is currently three scenes a day; Line Pro auto-renews, with a monthly and a yearly plan; prices are shown in the app and vary by country; refunds go through Apple. The terms also say to use Line for personal language practice. Whether sponsored content is within that is a question for the brief or the owner, and is not assumed here.
- Privacy says: microphone audio is streamed to the servers and relayed to speech services, not recorded or stored by Line; named providers include Google (Gemini Live), Soniox, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Clerk and RevenueCat.
- Conflicts to resolve before any number is used: the store listing lists weekly ($3.99), monthly ($9.99) and annual ($59.99) plans, while the terms name only monthly and yearly. The listing says fourteen languages, while one review says three. A review cites $300+ a year. Treat all of these as unverified.
- Avoid: fluency or "learn in X days" claims; quoting prices, free-scene counts or language counts; claiming to speak a language you do not; implying the AI character is a person.

## Areum (likely track)
- Listing: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/areum-science-based-ratings/id6756916007 . Site: https://areum.app/ . Terms: https://areum.app/legal/terms-conditions . Privacy: https://areum.app/legal/privacy-policy .
- Listing says: guided 3D face scan, report with a percentile rating and demographic perception breakdowns, trained on 7,251,000+ votes on AI-generated faces, informational and self-improvement only, subscription needed for the full report. The listing also makes data-handling statements; they are not repeated here because of the conflict below.
- Conflict: the store listing, the privacy policy and the terms disagree on where photos and scans go. The privacy policy says for a facial analysis the photograph is transmitted to servers, and the original photograph and numeric scores are retained; 3D geometry is processed and stored on device only. The terms say uploaded photos are deleted automatically after analysis. These three statements do not match each other, so make no claim at all about where photos or scans go, whether they are stored, kept or deleted, or whether anything stays on the device, even attributed to the listing.
- The site shows testimonials with unverifiable names. Do not reuse them.
