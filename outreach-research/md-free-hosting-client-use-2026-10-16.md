## The decision-changing distinction

**Vercel Hobby is limited to non-commercial personal use. Netlify says its free tier permits commercial projects.** That does not make Netlify operationally unlimited or justify moving any existing site without checking its requirements and the sender's decision.

### Vercel: client work is commercial even without a checkout

Current official fair-use guidelines:
https://vercel.com/docs/limits/fair-use-guidelines

Current Hobby documentation:
https://vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby

The published definition includes any deployment used for financial gain by anyone involved in producing the project, including a paid developer/consultant. Examples include advertising products/services and receiving payment to create, update or host a site. A site does not need to take payments itself to be commercial.

Hobby usage caps and the paid Pro/Enterprise permission are separate questions. Staying under the free bandwidth quota does not remove a commercial-use restriction. Conversely, a personal experiment can be legitimate on Hobby without becoming a paid subscription merely because it uses Vercel.

No claim is made about whether the sender's existing portfolio falls within the definition. Its content, purpose, plan and current deployment were not inspected in this research. No paid upgrade, migration, support enquiry or account warning was triggered by us. No token created, opened, copied or pasted.

### Netlify: commercial permission, but a hard-stop allowance

Netlify's own comparison guide:
https://www.netlify.com/guides/netlify-vs-vercel/

Current credit-based plan documentation:
https://docs.netlify.com/manage/accounts-and-billing/billing/billing-for-credit-based-plans/credit-based-pricing-plans


Free has 300 credits per month, a credit hard limit and no auto-recharge. Credits are usage units, not $300. Earlier v15 records the provider's all-project pause on a team when free credit is exhausted; separate add-ons/domains can still have costs. A hard stop protects from usage overage but can take a client site offline. It is not a reliability guarantee.

No host selected for the sender, workload estimated, domain transferred or deployment tested. Existing account/plan/usage and whether a specific site fits need a current check if the sender chooses hosting work.
