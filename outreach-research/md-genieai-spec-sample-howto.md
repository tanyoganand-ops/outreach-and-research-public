## Shot list (what to shoot or render)

1. Medium close-up, eye level, phone vertical, window light left. Stack of 3 printed contracts in foreground. Smile-less, direct. Quick push-in.
2. Over-the-shoulder or top-down laptop, PDF scrolling fast (speed ramp), counter text pops "12 pages / 40 clauses" with a quick swipe-in (Anton/bold sans, white on dark pill).
3. Screen recording at 1080x1920 crop of the app: upload step, then results list. 4 s upload + 1 s result. Cursor visible. If no real access, use a generic mock labelled "Example".
4. Three tight zooms (1.5 s each) on highlighted clauses, each with a coloured tag chip (amber = review, green = standard passes). Soft whoosh SFX on each pop.
5. Creator mid shot, gestures toward screen/second monitor. Caption lines kept to 5 words.
6. Creator to camera, end card slides up with the spec-sample label and handle placeholder [@yourhandle].

## Audio and edit
- Voice: natural, slightly fast, no radio voice. Record in a quiet room or car. Optional low lo-fi bed at -25 dB (royalty-free only).
- Cuts every 3-5 s, captions burned in, safe zone: keep text out of the bottom 15% and right 10%.
- Export 1080x1920, 30 fps, under 60 s.
