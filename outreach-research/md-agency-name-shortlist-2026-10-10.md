# Agency name shortlist - design / creative-coding portfolio

Researched 2026-10-10 (Europe/London). Checked live same day. 

## Key finding
- A short brandable .com is effectively gone. Of ~4,300 random 5-letter pronounceable strings I tested, 99.5% were registered; ~700 real-word + compound combos were 95% registered; every dictionary word tested (all 377 + 156 + 119) had a registered .com.
- Real dictionary words that are free at .co are almost all sold as **premium** ($546 to $5,458 per year). A DNS "does not exist" result is not proof of availability (tinct.co showed as free by DNS but is registered; skua.co, gorse.co etc. are premium). Prices below come from the Porkbun search page, not DNS.
- Practical route in this register: coined/rare word on .co/.studio/.design, plus .com only as a modifier variant (heytalve.com, talvestudio.com).

## Recommendation
**Talve** first, **Halvik** second. They are the only two where .co, .studio and .design are all ordinary-priced (about $38 for all three first year, about $120 a year after the sale), no design agency already uses the name, and Instagram, X and GitHub variants are free. Weakness: plain @talve is taken everywhere, so use @talvestudio (free on all three) or talvehq; and the .com is held by someone else.
Cost note: first-year prices are Porkbun promos; .studio renews at $41.71, .design at $46.86, .co at $31.20.

## Method and caveats
- .com: Verisign RDAP (registered = record exists). .studio/.design/.co: first screened by DNS, then confirmed on Porkbun's live search page for price and premium status (this is what caught the premium and "unavailable" cases).
- Conflict check is a web search for the name plus studio/agency/design; it is not a trademark search. UK IPO / EUIPO search is not done and should be before committing.
- No signups, purchases or outside contact were made.
- Not checked: .ai / .io / .xyz, other registrars (Namecheap, Cloudflare may differ slightly), Threads / LinkedIn / TikTok / Behance handles.
