## What was inspected (brand ad creative, static)
Sources: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dare-ai-photo-video/id6788347858 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/line-learn-any-language/id6780760864 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/regen-ai-dating-photos/id6757136097 (screenshots at is1-ssl.mzstatic.com: Dare screenshots 1, 3 and 5, Line 1, 3 and 5, ReGen 1 and 3, as listed in the assets SOURCES file; the others were not downloaded).

### Dare (brand ad)
- Pattern: big white headline at top, one phone mock below showing the real app screen. Headlines: "Create high quality AI Videos", "Edit, caption, translate and more", "Work on large scale projects".
- Product facts visible on screen: Quick Tools list (Create Video, Edit Studio, Swap Studio, Translate Studio, Caption Studio, Image Studio, Upscale).
- Transferable technique: one claim per frame; show the real screen that backs the claim; a menu or tools list works as a "what it does" beat in a screen demo.
- Care: the first screenshot shows a generated portrait of a person. Do not reuse or imitate. "Swap Studio" is a person-swap tool, so terms on permission for people and voices apply (see public claims note).

### Line (brand ad)
- Pattern: top headline, phone mock showing a story scene with a character, and a small badge reading "15+ languages available". Headlines: "Learn languages through stories", "Beginner to fluent levels", "Create your own stories".
- Conflict: the badge says 15+ languages. The listing text says fourteen. A review says three. Treat every language count as unverified.
- Transferable technique: a scene or character as the visual hook; a level picker as a "this adapts to you" beat; a story card list as proof of range.
- Care: "Beginner to fluent levels" is a brand claim. Do not repeat as a result.

### ReGen (brand ad)
- Pattern: grid of photo results with the caption "AI photos done right", then a generate screen with "Try hundreds of looks in minutes" and a credit counter (10/25/50 photos, "Generate for 25 credits").
- Visible: the app shows a credit cost per generation.
- Care: images show people in lifestyle photos. Do not copy or imitate, no looks or dating-success claims. Do not tell the sender to upload their own photos (terms: upload only your own photos; handled by the app flow only).
- Transferable technique: a results grid as the opening frame; a pre-generate screen showing the quantity choice as an honest "what happens next" beat.

## Brand ads versus creator opinions
- All the above is brand creative. None of it is creator opinion or campaign approval. Do not present these claims in a creator video as personal results.
