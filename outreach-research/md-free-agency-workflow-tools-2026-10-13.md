# Free agency workflow tools: 13 October 2026, 17:50 BST

Research only. No signup, installation, calendar connection, form publishing or application. New itemised leads beyond v11 and the checked free-stuff queue. Official pages read live; product output was not tested.

## Ranked new tools

1. **Squoosh: free private image compression.** Browser app, no signup shown, processing runs on the device and images are not sent to a server. Useful for reducing portfolio/client image weight before deployment. Open-source Apache 2.0; preserve required notices if redistributing software. The repository says Google Analytics receives basic visitor data and before/after file sizes, so "local images" does not mean zero telemetry. Open-to-all asset/software licence, no student requirement. Sources: https://squoosh.app ; https://github.com/googlechromelabs/squoosh ; https://github.com/GoogleChromeLabs/squoosh/blob/dev/LICENSE .

2. **Tally: free enquiry forms and client briefs.** Unlimited forms/submissions within fair use, conditional logic, calculations, file uploads, password protection and Sheets/Notion/Airtable integrations. Free upload limit is 10 MB per file. Tally branding stays; custom domain, removing branding, collaboration and custom CSS are paid. Can explore without an account, but publishing requires one. No minimum age was found in the terms read, which is not a confirmed under-18 grant. Fair-use examples include persistent 50,000 submissions/month, 100 GB uploads/month or 500 GB stored; this can lead to an offered custom paid plan, not unlimited infrastructure. Do not create or connect anything without the sender's yes. Avoid storing sensitive client data until permissions/data handling are checked. Sources: https://tally.so/pricing ; https://tally.so/help/fair-use-policy ; https://tally.so/help/terms-conditions .

3. **Screenity free Chrome extension: local demo recordings.** No account, watermark or recording-time limit advertised. Screen/window/tab capture, annotations, blur, camera bubble and local save. **13+**, guardian permission if local law requires it. Official terms dated 12 Oct 2026 distinguish free extension from paid cloud editor. Cloud storage, link sharing, advanced zoom/editing and templates are Pro; trial auto-converts unless cancelled. Use the extension page, not the paid homepage/trial. Extension is GPLv3; terms also prohibit recording/rendering "at commercial scale without a license", with scope undefined, so do not promise unrestricted bulk agency production. Personal demos/bug reports fit better than an automated commercial recording service. Not installed or tested. Sources: https://screenity.io/extension ; https://screenity.io/terms ; https://help.screenity.io/get-started/is-screenity-free-to-use ; https://github.com/alyssaxuu/screenity/ .

4. **Excalidraw open-source whiteboard.** Free browser canvas for wireframes, website flow diagrams and explaining a proposal; SVG/PNG export and realtime collaboration advertised by its repository. MIT licence permits commercial use with notices retained when copying software. Hosted Excalidraw+ cloud storage/workspace is separate and paid. Do not assume Plus or AI limits are included in the no-account canvas. Licence open to all, hosted-account age rules not checked. Sources: https://excalidraw.com ; https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw ; https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw/blob/master/LICENSE .

5. **Cal.com free individual scheduling, but 18+ only.** One user, unlimited event types/calendars and app integrations; team round-robin/shared scheduling and branding removal are paid. Scheduling service terms explicitly prohibit under-18 use. The merchandise site's 13+ terms are a different product and do not apply. Not a 16+ claim for the sender. Requires an account and calendar permissions, so no setup without approval. Sources: https://cal.com/pricing ; https://cal.com/terms .

## Best next choice

Squoosh is the most immediately useful new no-account tool for portfolio/client sites. Tally is the strongest future account-based agency tool. Screenity helps with demos but should stay local/free, with the commercial-scale caveat. Excalidraw is useful for planning rather than polished graphic design. Cal.com is only a future/18+ option.
