## How to read this pack

- VISIBLE ASK: a dated public post showing the company is paying for design capability. Two tiers:
  - CONTRACT-SHAPED: the company buys design on contract/freelance terms - the closest public signal to work a studio could supply.
  - IN-HOUSE HIRING ONLY: a permanent employee vacancy. This evidences investment in design, but the company is hiring an employee, not buying agency services. Labelled per row.
- A job post is evidence of demand, not an invitation to pitch services. All approaches below are proposals for sender to approve.
- SCREENED FIT: need is inferred from funding/launch activity, not expressed.
- INTERPRETATION LABEL: "product design" here is assumed to mean digital product design (UI/UX for apps and websites), NOT physical/industrial product design. sender to correct if wrong.
- Fit labels are screened/inferred, never validated buyer interest. No claim is made about client history either way. Drafts are PROPOSED and unsent; every capacity, service or offer phrase is bracketed for sender to confirm or strike.
- Honest market signal: public boards currently show mostly permanent employee vacancies and individual freelance gigs; open agency-supplier RFPs are rare in public. The contract-shaped asks are the closest public signal to agency-relevant demand found today.
