# Phone-only video editing walkthrough (Samsung / Android)

**Purpose:** edit a vertical UGC video start to finish on a Samsung phone, using only free tools, with no account sign-ups. All feature and paywall facts below were checked against live sources on 23 Oct 2026. App paywalls change often, so re-check at install time.

## 2. Install and setup (about 10 minutes)

1. Open **Google Play** and install **VN: Photo & Video Editor** (developer: VN / com.frontrow.vlog). It lists "Contains ads"; the review saw only a banner and a removable promo, not full-screen interruptions.
2. Open VN. Skip the optional survey. **No account is needed.** Do not sign in.
3. Permissions: allow access to **photos and videos only** when prompted. Deny contacts, notifications and anything else.
4. Before filming: wipe the lens, film **vertical 9:16** at 1080p or higher, and keep the phone steady (prop it up rather than handheld where possible).

---

## 3. Edit in VN, step by step

1. **New project.** Tap the big + button, pick your clips in rough order, tap Next.
2. **Set the canvas to 9:16.** Tap the aspect/format setting at the top of the preview and choose 9:16 so nothing gets cropped later.
3. **Rough cut.** Tap a clip, drag its ends to trim. Put the strongest moment (the hook) in the first 1-2 seconds. Split clips at pauses (tap the clip, then Split) and delete dead air.
4. **Pace it.** Short UGC should feel fast: cut on movement, remove "um" pauses, and keep most shots under 3 seconds unless the content needs longer.
5. **B-roll / overlay.** Use the PiP (picture-in-picture) or overlay option to layer product shots, screen recordings or reaction shots over the main clip. Pinch to resize and position.
6. **Text overlays.** Add text for the hook and key beats. Keep text away from the top, the bottom (where captions and buttons sit) and the right edge (where like/comment buttons sit on TikTok and Reels). These margins are practical guidance, not official platform specs; always preview the video in the real upload screen before posting to confirm nothing is covered. Use high-contrast text with a stroke or shadow so it reads over any background.
7. **Auto captions (free).** Tap Captions, then Auto captions. Choose the **Tiny or Base model**: VN's official page says these run on the phone itself, are free, and work offline. Avoid the cloud model (it costs credits and uploads your audio). **Review every line by hand afterwards**; auto captions always make mistakes. If the installed version asks for a sign-in or payment for captions, fall back to typing captions as manual text overlays (step 6); slower, but it works in every editor.
9. **Voiceover.** Record directly in VN, or record in Samsung's Voice Recorder app and import the file. A quiet room beats any effect.
10. **Transitions.** Hard cuts are the default for a reason. Use a transition only when it hides a jump; avoid flashy effects for client work.

## 4. Export settings that stay free

1. Tap **Export** and switch to the **Manual** tab (the Auto tab defaults to 1080p30).
2. For TikTok/Reels/Shorts: **1080p, 30 fps, bitrate around 10-16 Mbps** is plenty. The review's phone exported free up to 1440p at 60 fps; newer phones may offer more.
3. VN added **no watermark and no end card** in the review's test. Still, play the exported file end to end before posting.

---

## 5. If you use CapCut instead: paywall trap map

CapCut's free tier can still export clean video, but know these traps first:

- **Pro-badged content blocks export.** Any template, effect, font, music track, sticker or AI feature marked with a Pro badge triggers a subscribe prompt at export (CapCut's official help page). Fix: remove the badged item, or export with a watermark (not acceptable for paid work).
- **End card.** CapCut added a ~2-second black clip with its logo at the end of exports in the review's test. It is free to remove: delete that clip in the timeline before exporting.
- **Auto captions status is inconsistent.** CapCut's help page (Jan 2026) describes auto captions on mobile, but CapCut's own comparison page (Sep 2026) lists the auto caption generator as Pro-only. Treat it as unreliable on the free tier; if it demands Pro, do captions in VN instead.
- **Paid tiers: not covered here.** This guide is about free production. CapCut hides its rates until account creation and third-party price reports conflict, so if anything demands payment the free answer is VN, not a subscription.
- **Privacy:** PCMag (Aug 2026) flags that CapCut uploads content to ByteDance servers. If that matters, edit in VN and do not sign in anywhere.

## 6. Samsung-specific shortcuts

- **Quick single-clip edits:** Gallery > open video > Edit. Samsung's official support page (S20 and newer) lists trim, rotate, flip, text/captions, stickers, filters and volume control for video and music. Trims saved fast, kept the original resolution and added no watermark in the review's test. Good for cutting a clip to length or adding simple text before a full edit.
- **Screen recordings (for app demos):** swipe down the Quick Panel > Screen recorder > choose "Media sounds and mic" so your voice is recorded with the app audio.
- **Filming:** turn on the camera grid for framing, and prop the phone up rather than filming handheld where possible.

---

## 7. Pre-post checklist

- [ ] Watched the full export on the phone, once with sound and once muted, to confirm the captions carry the video on their own.
- [ ] No watermark and no end card.
- [ ] Hook lands inside the first 2 seconds.
- [ ] Music/sounds are rights-safe: commercial music library, own audio, or explicit commercial licence. In-app availability alone does not establish rights; when unclear, export voice-only.
- [ ] Ad/sponsored disclosure included where the law or platform rules require it (for example #ad). Disclosure rules are separate from the brand brief: verify the current rules for the platform and country you post in. Do not publish any test post without asking first.
