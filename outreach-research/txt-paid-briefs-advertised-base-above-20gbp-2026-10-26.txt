5. Where new paid briefs appear daily
      collabed.app/opportunities - 532 dated recruiting posts today (it collects public posts from Reddit UGC communities). New posts
      every few hours; filter by pay type. Original Reddit posts link out from each listing (Reddit itself blocks automated reading, so
      dates come from the Collabed pages).
      viral.app/creators/ugc-jobs - new fixed-base briefs most days; each brief states its own pay and country list. viral.app briefs pay
      through viral.app.
      talent.creatorland.com/jobs - mixed quality; check follower requirements and the platform's model before treating a listing as a
      brand deal.

  Read first. Pay figures are the posters' own advertised claims, quoted as stated - not verified payouts and not a promise of
  earnings or acceptance. Brands select who they hire; where a listing needs a test video, that test is unpaid application work. "No
  followers needed" and "no test video needed" are the posters' claims. Eligibility (location, language, casting asks) is not
  assessed; requirements shown are facts from each listing. Dollars converted at £1 = $1.3344 (26 Oct). Bonuses, per-view and "up
  to" amounts are never counted in the advertised fixed base. Check the weekly volume before applying - high-volume packages pay
  less per video than they first look.
