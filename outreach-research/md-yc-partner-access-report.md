# Getting in the room with YC partners (UK, young founder) - checked Mon 5 Oct 2026

## Bottom line
- The YC Winter 2027 application deadline is Mon 2 Nov 2026, 8pm PT (about 4am Tue 3 Nov UK). Decision by 11 Dec.

## Ranked routes
1. **Apply to YC (Winter 2027 or Early Decision).** Only route that guarantees a YC partner reads it. Deadline 2 Nov 2026, 8pm PT. Interviews Nov-Dec, mostly by video, with detailed feedback even on a rejection. Same form for Early Decision (select "A batch after Winter 2027"): accepted students get funded now and keep a later slot. YC says ~40% of funded companies are just an idea. Solo founders are accepted. I could not find a stated minimum age on YC's pages; the batch is in person in San Francisco (so visa/age/school is a real question to check before relying on it).
   - https://www.ycombinator.com/apply
   - https://www.ycombinator.com/early-decision
   - https://www.ycombinator.com/faq
2. **Watch YC's own event channels.** Dated YC events appear at https://events.ycombinator.com and https://www.ycombinator.com/events. On 5 Oct the events page showed only US events (listings from Aug-Dec, nothing in London). Recheck weekly; European tours have been announced only a few weeks ahead (the Jan 2026 stop was part of a five-day Europe tour).
   - https://www.imperialentrepreneurs.com/
   - Past event page: https://luma.com/pmgrwb4k and https://events.ycombinator.com/yc-imperial-2026 (page content did not load for me, so details not verified)
   - Felix write-up of the Jan visit: https://felixonline.co.uk/articles/y-combinator-on-campus/
4. **Imperial Enterprise Lab drop-in office hours.** Listed dates on the page were all in Aug-Sep (27 Aug, 16 Sep, 24 Sep); no October dates shown. Staff advice, not YC. Likely for Imperial people. https://www.imperialenterpriselab.com/events/enterprise-lab-office-hours/
5. **YC Startup School (free online).** Not partner access, but free YC lessons and a co-founder matching tool; the 2026 in-person event (San Francisco, 25-26 July) is past. https://www.startupschool.org/
6. **London startup events where YC alumni show up** (indirect, via founders who can intro). Example: Build Club / Agency Thursdays at Balderton, Thu 15 Oct 2026, 6-8pm, from a LinkedIn post ([personal LinkedIn URL withheld]). Not a YC event; I did not verify registration or age limits.
