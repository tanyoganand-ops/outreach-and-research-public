Agencies hiring creators & Bounty-style programmes - pay and entry evidence
DERIVED TEXT COPY of a 26 Oct 2026 research PDF (layout flattened to text; user-addressed framing removed). Figures are what the sources advertised, read 26 Oct 2026; nothing here means acceptance or that test work is paid. Research only; nothing was applied to or signed up for.
Filter used: UK or worldwide routes only, base above GBP 20 per video (above $26.69 at 1 GBP = $1.3344); commission-only, per-view-only and 'up to'-only offers are not counted as base. Count at the time: 1 route (JoinBrands) has a confirmed UK per-video base above GBP 20 net of its published fee; Demand Studios publishes rates above the bar but UK acceptance is unconfirmed (section 1). 2 more let creators set their own rate (section 2). 8 are hiring but publish no creator rates (section 3). Section 4 ranks them by ease of entry.

4. Ease of entry, ranked - with the catch for each
Ranked by how little stands between a creator and a first paid brief, using only the entry evidence on each row. Easy signup and easy
earnings are different things; the catch is stated per rank.
1. Collabstr - No application review is mentioned on its creator page - you list services and brands buy at your listed price. Caveat:
easy to join is not easy to earn; brands must choose your listing.
2. JoinBrands - Free signup and every job shows its exact payout before you apply, so there is no guessing; you still need each
brand's approval per job, and the free plan takes 20%.
3. Influee - Free app, profile from existing samples; its own stat says 83% of new creators land a first paid project within 14 days
(advertised claim, not a promise).
4. The UGC Agency - One-minute application, claims matching within an hour, UK-registered with a stated UK creator network.
5. Demand Studios - No follower bar and published rates, but it asks the most commitment: confident on camera, 24-hour raw
delivery, at least 4 shoots per month.
6. UK rosters: hypjam, Nonsensical, UGC Agent, TapLab - Small human-reviewed rosters; entry is a short form plus your on-
camera examples; how selective they are is not published.
7. Insense (UGC-only) - Highest quality bar here: a manual portfolio review of your existing work before you see campaigns.
8. Billo - Signup is easy and UK is eligible, but you cannot see what tasks pay until you are inside.
9. Vidsy - Every invited creator gets paid (its FAQ), but creator forums describe briefs as competitive to win.

 Read first. Pay figures are each site's own advertised claims, quoted as stated - not verified payouts, earnings promises, or a

 view, US-only platforms, individual job posts, and talent agencies for established creators were checked and filtered out. Nothing
