# Outreach and research archive (public copy)

Public copy of P's recovered research deliverables, assembled 22 November 2026. The complete archive, including exam-derived and private-labelled material, stays in a separate private backup; nothing was deleted.

Everything here is a dated snapshot (late October - 22 November 2026). Leads, pay figures, programme terms, vacancies and deadlines were correct when checked and may have changed. Nothing here is proof that any message, application or pitch was sent. Placeholders in [square brackets] are unfilled. Older versions are kept alongside newer ones on purpose.
